Appendix B. SRE Team Charter Template

<Team Name> Charter

Status: <Draft | In Review | Approved | Obsolete>
Last updated: <date>
Author(s): <author email address(es)>
Reviewer(s): <relevant stakeholders>
Approver: <senior SRE exec>

Mission

A nonconfidential one-liner that everyone on the team and stakeholders agree describes the team’s role in the context of the broader organization.

Scope

Describe the technology environment, customers, and team offerings (engagement model, special expertise, etc.), as well as elements that are specifically out of scope.

Services

Describe the (group of) services the team supports to further clarify its scope.

Success

Based on the intended scope, describe what success would look like. This should illustrate how the team achieves and maintains its goals over time.

Values

Describe the culture of the team and how this helps it and its partners be successful, sustainable, and healthy.
